Privacy Policy – Oakline Studio

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

Oakline Studio is a private, internal tool operated by Oakline Media. It is used only by Oakline Media's owners, with Oakline Media's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • Google Drive: files in Oakline Media's own Drive folders (scripts, audio, images and videos for our channels), to read, create and update production files.
  • YouTube: Oakline Media's own channels, to upload our own videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules.

The tool does not access the data of any other person or account.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce, organise and publish Oakline Media's own content. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately in Oakline Media's own secured cloud environment and are never published. Files remain in Oakline Media's own Google Drive.

Google API Services

Oakline Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
